Joining the group

I advise PhD students in the Statistics, Bioinformatics and Computational Biology, and Data Science programs at WPI, master’s students in Applied Statistics and Bioinformatics and Computational Biology, and undergraduates through MQPs, IQPs and independent studies. If our research interests you, email me at zheyangwu@wpi.edu with a short note on your background and what you would like to work on. The Teaching page says how projects run.
